# Format example (found footage)

> **[COLOR GRADE]**
> Color grade: washed-out camcorder colour, crushed blacks, cold green cast

## SHOT 1 — The Hook (0:00–0:04)

**[STILL]** A dark forest clearing at night, shot from a handheld camcorder held at chest height. A small orange tent glows from inside. A woman's silhouette crouches by the entrance. Camera light on the ground in front of her.

**[MOTION]** The camera wobbles as the operator walks closer. The woman looks up into the lens and whispers. Her breath is visible. Focus drifts onto the tent behind her.

**[CHARACTERS]** Mia

**[DIALOGUE]** Did you hear that?

**[SFX]** Wind in the trees, a distant owl, nylon fabric rustling, the operator breathing close to the mic.

**[BEAT]** hook

## SHOT 2 — The Knock (0:04–0:09)

**[STILL]** Inside the tent, lit only by a flashlight. A woman holds the camera on herself, eyes wide. Behind her the tent wall shows the shadow of a very tall thin figure standing outside, perfectly still.

**[MOTION]** She slowly turns the camera toward the tent wall. The shadow does not move. A knock sounds from the fabric.

**[CHARACTERS]** Mia

**[SFX]** A soft knock on the fabric. Her held breath. Nothing else.

**[BEAT]** build
